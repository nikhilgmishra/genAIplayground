Retain application tiers A0–A3, explicitly not NIST certification:
A0 public information, neutral unverified intake and human/safety routing.
A1 approved possession verification, only tenant-approved low-risk operations.
A2 account authentication through existing portal with MFA/passkey, bound to call.
A3 recent transaction-bound step-up and explicit approval for consequential actions.

Implement real OIDC issuer discovery/validation, aud/iss/exp/nonce checks, PKCE where

to a trusted carrier interaction AND session generation; never infer binding from

The verified identity service supplies canonical actor, selected role, authorized
subject, organization, scopes, relationship, assurance, authentication time, consent
or other permitted processing basis, expiry and revocation. Caller ID, DOB, NPI,
address, email domain, claim details, voice familiarity and CDP profile are not
authenticators. No account-existence disclosure before authorization. [L04]

If OTP is configured, use pre-registered channels, isolated portal/DTMF capture,
one use, short expiry, throttling and recovery. A five-minute expiry, three attempts
per challenge and five per account/hour are engineering starting values pending
identity-owner approval. Do not expose digits to ASR, models, recordings or logs.
Pausing one recorder is insufficient. Never send verification to a new caller-
supplied destination and use that alone as account proof. Portal verification must
work even if secure DTMF is unavailable. No passwords/card secrets through models.

Provider/practice access requires authoritative active delegation. Partners/payers
require verified affiliation, contract, purpose and person-level scope. Benefit
sponsorship alone never authorizes an employer to learn treatment status. Minors,
guardians and representatives require record-specific current authority; ambiguous
rights go to a specialist. Never union multiple roles silently.

Implement default-deny OPA decisions plus row/field checks in storage and adapters.
Use separate integration identities per environment and capability. Service-to-
service authentication does not authenticate the caller. Workforce SSO/MFA roles:
support agent, supervisor, safety responder, auditor, knowledge editor, policy
approver, deployment operator and security administrator. Break-glass is human-
controlled, time-bound and audited, not a tool the model can self-authorize.

Keep recording consent, transcription/AI processing permission, support purpose,
data disclosure authority, notification subscription and marketing consent distinct.
Explicit opt-out, unknown, expired and affirmative permission are separate states.
Implement revocation propagation to sessions, caches, memory and pending work.

7. CONTEXT ENGINE: HISTORY PLUS LIVE TRUTH

"Full history" means a permissioned, searchable support timeline with explicit
coverage and gaps. It does not mean dumping all company records into an LLM or
retrieving psychotherapy notes. Implement four layers:
1. Canonical support timeline from permitted calls, messages, cases, tasks, source
   transactions, authorized app interactions and verified preferences.
2. Low-latency current-state projections built from authenticated event ingestion.
3. Fresh authoritative lookups for volatile facts and before any consequential act.
4. Task-specific ContextSnapshot assembled with current identity/purpose/ACL filters,
   provenance, freshness budgets, contradictions and omissions.

Represent a ContextFact with source_system, source_record_id, source_version,
event_id, occurred_at, effective_at/valid interval, source_updated_at, received_at,
observed_at, classification, authority_rank, verified/caller_reported/inferred
status, expiry, allowed purposes and evidence reference. Do not let ingestion time
overwrite business effective time. Maintain supersession/retraction, deletion and
correction lineage. Model-generated summary claims link to their supporting facts.

Entity resolution:
- Versioned canonical IDs with authoritative cross-system links and relationship
  validity intervals. Keep ambiguous/unmatched identities quarantined.
- No fuzzy email/name/phone matching, shared-device linking, CDP alias, probabilistic
  identity or LLM inference can authorize protected-data merging/disclosure.
- Deterministic enrolled links and current authority govern joins. Merges/splits
  require reviewed evidence, invalidate affected caches, and support reversible
  lineage without cross-account leakage. The identity graph itself is sensitive.

Ingestion and history reconciliation:
- Authenticate ingress; persist an encrypted durable inbox before acknowledging;
  deduplicate by source/tenant/event identity; process at least once with consumer
  idempotency. Record payload digest, schema and actual auth mechanism.
- Preserve sequence/source version where available; tolerate duplicates, reorder,
  delayed clocks, replay and backfill. Never use arrival order alone for recency.
- Quarantine unknown schemas/fields according to explicit contracts and alert; do
  not silently promote unclassified fields into context. Keep approved raw replay
  artifacts in restricted storage, not ordinary logs.
- Track source low/high watermarks, checkpoint gaps, expected/delivered counts,
  snapshot+delta boundaries and periodic reconciliation. A backfill must not roll
  back a newer live state. Source silence may be no activity or a broken feed;
  distinguish with heartbeats and reconciliation, not guesses.
- Push newly accepted relevant events to the active conversation through versioned
  context updates. Invalidate stale proposed answers and pending confirmations.
  An unrelated new event must not unnecessarily restart the whole conversation.

Context assembly:
- Start protected context only after authorization. Prefetch public knowledge or
  already-authorized reads; no speculative mutations and no pre-auth PHI model load.
- Select current issue, relevant past interactions, promises, unresolved tasks,
  recent transactions and preferences with a token/latency budget. Retrieval must
  be both policy-filtered before search and checked again on return.
- Provide a small timeline summary plus explicit facts and citations; allow bounded
  drill-down. Report covered sources, unavailable sources, watermarks, truncation,
  conflict and omission reasons. Never label a partial snapshot "complete".
- Resolve field-specific source precedence by signed configuration. Surface material
  contradictions and query authoritative systems; do not average statuses or choose
  whichever source agrees with the caller. Show staff why a fact was selected.
- Example starting SLOs, subject to tenant validation: accepted event -> projection
  p95 <=5 seconds under healthy input; assembled cached context p95 <=200 ms; use
  source-specific max ages. Authorization, consent revocation and mutation state
  require current enforcement; a five-second generic cache is not sufficient.
- A warehouse snapshot that predates a recent payment must trigger live verification
  or an honest unknown, never a false claim that payment was not made.

Cache keys include tenant, subject, role, purpose, policy/relationship version and
data revision. Prevent cross-user cache hits, unbounded transcript storage and stale
authorization reuse. Export timelines under scoped permission; implement correction,
access and deletion workflows covering derivatives, embeddings and caches.

8. KNOWLEDGE, MEMORY AND GROUNDING

Ship production ingestion and management UI, not just an embeddings function.
Each article/runbook has owner, audience, locale, jurisdiction, allowed intents,
source URL/record, version, effective date, expiry, sensitivity, access rules and
approval. Parse and scan safely; defend against malicious instructions, URLs,
attachments, oversized files and index poisoning. Unapproved content is draft.

Use PostgreSQL full-text + pgvector hybrid retrieval with a qualified reranker.
Evaluate recall@k, ranking, citation accuracy, source-version accuracy and negative
retrieval; tune per domain. Keep structured transaction truth in source tools.
Expensive graph retrieval is optional only when measured gains justify it. A vector
similarity score is not a confidence score or authorization decision. [R12]

Approved public FAQ and protected operational knowledge must be distinguishable.
Conflicting, missing, expired or wrong-jurisdiction policies cause a clarification,
fresh read or owned escalation. No citation laundering: every material factual
assertion must resolve to an allowed source span/field or an actual effect receipt.

Persistent memory is tenant-owned and exportable. Store useful support preferences,
open issues, promises, completed actions and reviewed summaries, with provenance,
expiry and verification labels. Separate caller reports from source facts. Do not
infer diagnoses, vulnerability, personality, lifetime value-based service rights,
or therapy guidance. Do not persist raw hidden model reasoning. Memory cannot grant
permission, establish medical facts, or override current sources or consent.

Support memory editing/correction with history, purpose limits and deletion. Do not
build a cross-customer transcript index or unrestricted nightly self-rewriting
memory. Tenant-isolated semantic caches require the same disclosure checks.

9. MODEL HARNESS AND QUALIFIED MODEL PORTFOLIO

10. VOICE: NATURAL CONVERSATION WITH AN ENFORCED AUDIO BOUNDARY

Build actual media ingress/egress, codec conversion, WebRTC/SIP bridging, ASR,
endpointing, interruptions, queueing, playback receipts and call lifecycle. A
browser animation or transcript replay is not a telephone implementation.

Naturalness requirements:
- Identify the company and automated support role, accurately disclose processing/
  recording, and offer a human. Do not claim to be human, a therapist or a clinician.
- Warm, calm, short, concrete language; one question at a time; no apology loops,
  forced cheerfulness, manipulative attachment or invented feelings.
- Remember authorized information already given; clarify ambiguity instead of
  restarting intake. Read names/numbers/dates/currency carefully. Confirm timezone.
- Distinguish backchannels, background speech and meaningful interruption. Support
  long pauses, stuttering, speech impairment, relay users, slower playback, repeats,
  DTMF navigation and an interpreter route. No diagnostic inference from prosody.
- Return to legitimate work after an attack/refusal when safe. Do not punish the
  caller with endless refusal or authentication for public service information.
- Acknowledgments are brief and truthful; "checking" requires actual work. Do not
  mask a stall with filler or count filler as useful-answer latency.

SpeechPlan contains utterance_class, exact text or bounded allowed paraphrase,
source/receipt refs, protected slots, policy/auth/context versions, session/turn,
expiry, and output-decision digest. Enforce before ANY applicable audio escapes:
1. Validate authorization and source freshness.
2. Validate facts, amounts, dates, outcome language, scope and allowed wording.
3. Render using the permitted path.
4. Check session generation/turn/cancellation again at enqueue and emission.
5. Record approved content/audio digest and actual emitted/playback status.

Use fixed clinician/legal-approved wording for safety, identity, disclosure and
material commitments; deterministic trusted slots for financial/account facts.
Permit bounded grounded paraphrase for low-risk explanation. Independent TTS can
still mispronounce: qualify exact readouts and use pronunciation/number controls,
listen-back tests and repetition when necessary. Do not claim mathematical audio
fidelity from a transcript judge.

Native audio generated behind a buffer must be checked against authorized source
facts and exact protected slots. A judge alone is not an authorization boundary;
if the mode cannot prevent unapproved speech, it cannot speak protected responses.
Actual audio, model transcript and independent ASR transcript are separate evidence.

Interruptions invalidate pending speech and ambiguous confirmations, increment
turn revisions and flush provider AND local playback buffers. Cancelling speech
does not prove a business action was cancelled; reconcile any dispatched action.
Late tool results update a still-authorized case but may not speak into a new turn.
Call-owner leases and fencing prevent two replicas from speaking or committing.
Test worker crash, reconnect, session expiry, participant changes and rolling deploy.
Ephemeral audio frames need not survive crash; authority, case state and accepted
actions must. Recovery starts a correctly bound session or a human route.

Have independently available, licensed, versioned fallback audio for model, ASR,
TTS and judge failures. A generative model must not generate its own last-resort
safety/error instruction. Keep the telephony route usable when the whole AI service
is down. Never leave a failed safety response as silence.

Measure end-of-caller-speech to first meaningful handset audio, actual barge-in
onset to last audible bot audio, plus detection latency separately. Timestamp ASR,
reasoning, tools, validation, synthesis, buffers and network. Cached clips and a
fast local loop do not establish PSTN latency. Section 17 specifies targets.

11. CONTROLLED ACTIONS AND INDEPENDENT EFFECT VERIFICATION

Preserve prepare -> confirm -> commit -> reconcile, and extend it across real
connectors. Model proposals cannot set actor, target authority, fees or success.

PreparedAction includes source-grounded target, operation, exact parameters,
before-state/version, impact/fees, policy decision, expiration, preconditions and
canonical digest. Present material consequences. ConfirmationReceipt binds a fresh
affirmation on a trusted channel to that digest, actor, session and expiry. Neither
an earlier "yes", provider approval for another subject, nor model-generated
confirmation is usable. Material parameter changes require a new confirmation.

Commit atomically records durable intent, unique action/idempotency identity,
authorization and outbox before dispatch; rechecks current authority, source state,
confirmation and policy. Enforce optimistic concurrency, action ownership leases,
fencing and uniqueness. Retries use the same provider key, not a new action.

Track NOT_STARTED, DISPATCHED, COMMITTED, FAILED, UNKNOWN and reconciled states.
A timeout may occur after effect. Reconcile using authoritative lookup, external
key or receipt before retrying. "Not found" is not definitive before a provider's
documented visibility window. For unreconcilable operations route to a human;
never guess or retry an uncertain non-idempotent effect.

EffectVerification is independent of the model and response text: query the source
or validate a trusted completion event, check exact target/new state/version, and
record receipt/evidence. For eventual consistency, report accepted/pending until
verified. Compensation requires domain policy and current authority; it is not a
generic inverse call. Reconcile case state and external state after restart.

Allow synchronous approved actions within voice deadlines. For long-running work,
issue a narrow ActionGrant tied to actor/subject/purpose, exact prepared action or
explicitly enumerated permitted steps, parameter bounds, maximum uses, expiry,
revocation and approval evidence. It does not reuse an expired call token or waive
fresh confirmation for a different consequential transaction. Recheck before each
disclosure/effect and ask through the secure companion when material change needs
new confirmation. Suspended work retains an owner and deadline.

Support these outcome labels per issue, not just per call:
RESOLVED_WITH_EVIDENCE / INFORMATION_PROVIDED / REQUEST_ACCEPTED /
WAITING_FOR_SYSTEM / WAITING_FOR_HUMAN / WAITING_FOR_CALLER /
TRANSFERRED_WITH_ACCEPTANCE / DECLINED_BY_POLICY / CALLER_DISCONNECTED /
FAILED / OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. Preserve reopened and partially resolved states.

12. DURABLE CASE OWNERSHIP AND LONG-HORIZON WORK

Implement all existing workflows and the catalogue in Section 20 through production
drivers. Cases survive hang-up, model replacement, worker crash and deployment.
Every nonterminal case/task has owner, next action, deadline, retry budget, escalation
route and last verified progress. No abandoned task or endless polling loop.

Durable workflow states include ACCEPTED, VALIDATING, INVESTIGATING,
WAITING_FOR_SYSTEM/HUMAN/CALLER, READY_TO_COMMIT, VERIFYING_EFFECT,
COMPLETED, FAILED, CANCELLED and EXPIRED. Record versioned transitions and causal
events. Use authenticated human approvals, timers, vendor events, bounded polling,
backoff, timeouts, cancellation, reconciliation and domain-approved compensation.
Do not expose workflow task tokens in links or let a model complete human tasks.

Billing disputes, claim investigations, provider document/remittance issues,
referral-status investigation and partner onboarding need real steps and named
owners, not one timer that ends by closing a Case. Source status, a human decision,
customer confirmation and verified downstream effect remain distinguishable.

Link repeated contact to the right issue through deterministic relationships and
reviewable matching. Do not merge different people's cases based on similar text.
Handle concurrent calls, multiple issues, abandoned contacts, duplicate CRM records
and new evidence after resolution. A resolved case may reopen; preserve history.

Long-horizon plans have executable bounded steps drawn from approved capabilities.
Replanning may change investigation, not self-expand authority. Retain the policy
snapshot for explanation but apply current revocations, legal constraints and
relationship changes. Workflow version migrations require compatibility tests and
explicit in-flight migration strategy. Step Functions' lifetime/history limits must
not truncate the durable case record; use continuation/checkpoint patterns as needed.

Notifications: neutral content and secure links, registered channels, preferences,
quiet hours, frequency/contact caps, STOP/opt-out and revocation. Delivery is not
receipt or resolution. Outbound calls require separately activated country/recipient,
purpose, consent/exemption, caller ID, recording, budget and staffing settings.
Inbound permission is not blanket outbound consent. Public emergency lines must
never receive synthetic tests. [L05]

13. SAFETY, ACCESSIBILITY AND ACCEPTED HUMAN HANDOFF

Implement semantic routing signals plus deterministic rules and caller/human
requests. Cover explicit and indirect concerns, negation, past/third-party speech,
idioms, uncertain ASR and supported languages. Do not label every frustrated caller
an emergency. Classifier failure/uncertainty uses an approved fallback, never a
silent fail-open into unrestricted medical conversation.

On a validated or unresolved safety concern: pause ordinary administrative work,
invalidate pending nonessential actions, play the approved fixed acknowledgment,
and initiate the appropriate human route. No authentication/payment prerequisite.
No diagnosis, clinical risk assessment, therapy, medication guidance or autonomous
emergency determination. The clinical safety owner defines immediate-danger,
ambiguous, third-party, refusal, disconnect, after-hours and unavailable-responder
procedures, including lawful disclosures and jurisdictions.

Route selection comes from trusted allowlisted queues/destinations and actual
service hours/capacity. Do not dial arbitrary user/model/document-supplied numbers.
External emergency/crisis contact requires approved operational protocol, geography,
consent or legal basis, minimum disclosure and a failed-connection procedure.

Transfer events: REQUESTED, QUEUED, RECEIVER_ACCEPTED, BRIDGED,
CALLER_CONFIRMED_CONNECTED and COMPLETED, plus timeout/failure/disconnect. Persist
authenticated provider events with event IDs and causal timestamps. Accept duplicate
and out-of-order delivery into a durable inbox; derive state only when prerequisites
are evidenced. A late event cannot rewrite a failed historical audio connection
into success. Do not require the network to deliver events in your ideal order.

Verify receiver identity/queue eligibility, receipt of HandoffPackage, human
acceptance and carrier/media connection evidence. A self-reported model "connected"
or a successful dialing command is insufficient. Keep caller on the line during a
warm transfer; use correct hold/media mixing and measure both legs. If unavailable,
retain ownership, give truthful status and follow the next approved staffed route.

HandoffPackage contains permitted issue summary, assurance and its limits, subject/
role scope, consent, attempted actions/receipts, outstanding tasks, support/Case
reference, language/accommodation and minimum necessary routing context. Avoid
making callers repeat information already delivered. A human can decline; record
that and route explicitly instead of abandoning the case.

The priority-route initiation target is <=2 seconds AFTER validated signal; also
measure time from actual utterance to detection. A proposed <=30-second staffed
connection is a separate operations commitment, not an LLM guarantee. Expiring
timers, refusal counters, cost limits and max turns must never silently abandon
the safety flow. An independent telephony continuity route remains available.

independent attestation, including operating-period evidence for Type II. Neither

Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity and Privacy criteria
where applicable. Do not invent control IDs or claim every category is in scope.

keep current: HIPAA, HITECH/breach duties, 42 CFR Part 2 where applicable, separate
psychotherapy/SUD-counseling-note restrictions, state mental-health/confidentiality

Unknown jurisdictions do not silently inherit the least restrictive policy.

HHS states the Part 2 final-rule compliance date was February 16, 2026; applicability
is program/record-specific. Do not label all mental-health records Part 2, or treat
special notes as ordinary support context. Exclude therapy notes/transcripts,
diagnoses, medication histories and clinical scores from default support retrieval.
Volunteered clinical speech still makes the actual audio processing path sensitive.
US-only deployment is a configurable organizational default, not a claim that HIPAA
categorically requires US hosting. [L01,L02,L03,L06]

Data protection implementation:
- Inventory every processor, model, subprocessor, region, storage/index/cache,
  support-access path, backup, telemetry sink and deletion path. Contract coverage,
  BAA, permitted purpose and actual feature/endpoint settings must be evidenced.
- Separate transient conversation, support business records, restricted QA media,

  raw payloads to general telemetry or judges. Formal de-identification needs its

- No PHI/secrets in metric labels, trace baggage, resource names, URLs, exception
  text, alert payloads, CI logs or ordinary support tickets. Restricted investigation
  records use opaque IDs and short-lived permission-checked evidence retrieval.
- Encryption at rest/in transit, private networking, egress allowlists, KMS grants
  scoped to service/resource/context, key rotation, workload identities, short-lived

- Separate key administration, application use, audit writing and audit reading.

- Implement deletion across transcripts, audio, projections, summaries, embeddings,

clinical actions or deploy its unsecured test environment. [R01,R02,R03,R09,R12]

SCORER REPAIR IS REQUIRED:
Rerun and investigate historical FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE cases. Distinguish actual product
defect, fixture defect, impossible task and unmet precondition with evidence. A fault
scenario passes only if the intended failure path was reached AND its postconditions
were asserted. If a crash-after-commit injection never reached commit, it is NOT
proof of crash safety. Preserve the original failing result and corrected rerun.
Do not remove hard cases, relabel unmet assertions, or weaken gates to get green.
Keep a fixed required denominator; skipped/NOT_RUN/INCONCLUSIVE cases do not count
as PASS. Report coverage holes and unsupported intents explicitly.

Maintain development, tuning, held-out acceptance and regression corpora, with
lineage and splits at scenario family/account/conversation level to prevent leakage.
Use synthetic or licensed/consented data by default. Real support data requires
approved processing/minimization/retention, never copying PHI to normal CI or a
consumer judge. Separate synthetic expected labels from actual human review.

Initial launch suite:
- >=50 reviewed support intents across clients/providers/partners, each with eight
  variant families: baseline, frustrated, indirect/ambiguous, interrupted/noisy,
  verification failure, malicious instruction, dependency failure, repeat/handoff.
- >=500 human-reviewed privacy/authorization/clinical-boundary/safety-routing cases
  separate from routine quality. Generate candidates now; mark human review PENDING
  until genuine reviewers act. Do not synthesize review signatures.
- Repeat selected stochastic acceptance cases >=5 times; measure all-k reliability
  as well as pass@1. Do not report best-of-many as first-attempt success.
- Include shared devices, proxies/minors, role switching, culturally varied speech,
  speech impairment, relay/interpreter use, long pauses and out-of-distribution
  intents. Do not infer protected traits from live audio for analytics.

FAULT MATRIX:
Auth expires while idle/during speech/after prepare; revoked delegation/consent;
parallel calls; changed slot during confirmation; stale source/contradictory event;
out-of-order/duplicate/missing webhook; provider accepts then times out; replica dies
after commit; lost/late receipt; CRM down; drifted schema; full DLQ; model refusal/
hang/overload; ASR/TTS/judge failure; human unavailable/declines; dropped bridge;
recording refusal; audit sink/local persistence failure; redaction failure;
source deletion/merge/split; region failover and vendor quota/budget exhaustion.

INPUT AND OUTPUT MONITORS:
Monitor ASR entity accuracy, uncertainty, language support, intent routing, prompt
injection, scope, data minimization, retrieved-source relevance, stale/unsupported
facts, commitments, authorization, false completion/transfer, stigma/sycophancy,
loops, inappropriate reassurance and safety-routing outcomes. Use deterministic
checks for facts/actions plus qualified semantic judges. Evaluate each model input
boundary and each output boundary, including tool and TTS outputs where relevant.

Judges themselves need test sets, blind human labels, severity definitions, precision/
recall, sensitivity/specificity with confidence intervals, calibration and drift
checks. Include a random sample of unflagged conversations to estimate missed cases;
only reviewing flagged calls cannot establish population safety. Separate generator
and judge model families when feasible; agreement is not independent truth. Human
adjudication resolves material disagreements. Judge scores cannot prove external
effects, caller authority or an accepted human connection. [R06,V04,V06]

Acceptance targets are engineering proposals, not legal thresholds or measurements:
- Usable support routing availability >=99.95% monthly, end to end.
- Warm simple turn: end of caller speech -> first meaningful handset audio p50
  <=800 ms, p95 <=1.5 s. Optimization goal p50 <=500 ms, p95 <=1 s, without weakened
  controls. Separate cold starts, acknowledgments and tool-backed turns.
- Healthy-dependency tool-backed meaningful response p95 <=3 s.
- Interruption stop p95 <=250 ms from detected interruption to last handset audio;
  separately report actual caller-interruption onset -> detection -> stop.
- 100% protected actions/utterances have valid checks; all accepted consequential
  actions have durable audit; zero duplicate effects in required failure tests.
- CRM synchronization >=99.9% within 60 s under normal dependencies; report outage
  backlog and recovery separately. Event/context targets from Section 7 also apply.
- >=99.5% accepted transfer attempts connect when a receiver is available. Also
  publish ALL attempts, unavailable/declined receivers and failed connections.
- >=95% verified goal completion per supported routine launch intent under declared
  conditions; no critical failure. Do not blend unsupported requests into a fake
  success metric or hide failed supported attempts with eligibility relabeling.
- Human warmth/clarity >=4/5 baseline, 4.3/5 optimization goal; predeclare rubric,
  reviewer count, uncertainty and unacceptable subgroup outcomes before testing.
- Any critical privacy/authorization/clinical-boundary/effect failure blocks the
  affected release, regardless of high average scores or low cost.

Publish p50/p95/p99 by intent/language/audience/assurance/model/voice/load/region;
sample counts, exclusions, missing observations and confidence intervals. Report
ASR important-entity error as well as WER. Include completion latency, total silence,
interruptions, backchannel selectivity and user effort. Finite zero observed errors
never proves zero risk; ~3/n is only a rough upper 95% bound under the relevant
independence assumptions. Repeated related simulations aren't n independent people.

EvaluationResult records scenario/run/seed/split, expected scope and initial world,
all version digests, requested/reached fault, observed tools, deterministic checks,
actual final source state, audio evidence, privacy/safety/auth results, human/judge
labels and disagreements, latency, cost, outcome, reviewer, errors and evidence refs.
Use append-only results and reproducible rerun commands.

18. CONTROLLED SELF-IMPROVEMENT

Build a continuous improvement service and UI: collect approved observations ->
detect failure clusters/drift -> propose changes -> replay/evaluate -> review ->
canary -> measure -> promote or rollback. It should automatically do the analysis
and prepare useful changes, not merely email charts. [V01,V04,V06]

ChangeProposal binds evidence, suspected cause, exact prompt/knowledge/model/tool/
configuration diff, affected capabilities, expected benefit, risks, tests and
rollback. Distinguish verified cause from hypothesis. Candidate generation and
offline optimization may run autonomously in a bounded approved environment.
No self-promotion of production behavior, self-editing permissions, unrestricted
online RL, or training directly on unreviewed production conversations.

Support retrieval tuning, prompt optimization, router calibration, approved knowledge
updates and task-specific fine-tuning/LoRA recipes where measurable evidence warrants
them. Training data needs provenance, license, consent/purpose, minimization, leakage
checks and retention. A Hugging Face clinical label or therapist-style dataset is
not approval. Include memorization/extraction and bias tests for trained artifacts.
Fine-tuning is not necessary for a complete initial product when a qualified model
and harness meet the contract; ship runnable recipes and evaluation/promotion gates.

Keep held-out tests inaccessible to optimizer feedback; refresh tests under review.
Review all detected critical events and pilot safety transfers, plus stratified and
random routine samples. Evaluate the monitors continuously. Track repeat contact
24h/7d, reopens, unresolved promises, inappropriate handoff suppression and complaints.
Optimize verified outcomes AND naturalness; do not reward containment at all costs.

Canaries have signed limits by tenant/cohort/intents/language, sample/period targets,
error budget and automatic rollback thresholds. Cohort assignment is stable and
auditable; current permissions always apply. Experiment only within approved safe
behavior. No experimentation on crisis instructions, consent or authority controls
without separately governed review. Manual and automatic containment preserve
human/safety routing. Record releases and in-flight workflow compatibility.
