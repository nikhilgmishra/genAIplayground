# WP-05 proposed threat rows: caller and workforce identity live path

Drafted by WP-05 (identity) for the phase B threat-model owner to merge into
[`docs/security/threat-model.md`](../../threat-model.md). Row ids are provisional (`T-FO-05-nn`); the merging
owner assigns the next `T-` numbers. The boundary is the sign-in of operators to the console (workforce IdP),
the binding of callers to an identity (caller IdPs), the sessions both produce, break-glass access and the
recovery path when the workforce IdP is unavailable. Evidence is Layer 1 (unit tests over real modules and
PGlite) and Layer 2 (Keycloak 26 over TLS, postgres:16, DynamoDB Local); nothing runs against a tenant IdP
(NOT_CONFIGURED).
